# W-007 Job loop and processor contract

- **Build:** the `Processor` protocol, a registry, claiming with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, leases, retry with backoff, and dead-lettering after the attempt limit.
- **Done when:** two workers never claim the same job, a crashed worker's lease expires and the job reruns, an always-failing processor ends dead after the limit, and a rerun creates no duplicate rows.
- **Not now:** the GPU branch.
